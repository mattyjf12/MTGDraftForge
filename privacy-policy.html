MTG Draft Forge — Privacy Policy

Effective date: May 2026

MTG Draft Forge ("the App") is a Magic: The Gathering tournament and draft management tool for iOS and Android. This policy explains what data we collect, why, and how it is stored.

1. Information We Collect

  • Display name — the name you choose when you first open the app. This is stored locally on your device and synced to our servers only when you join or create a tournament room.
  • Tournament data — room names, player lists, match results, and standings. This data is shared in real time with other players in the same room.
  • Avatar URL — an optional image URL you provide. We do not host or upload images; we only store the URL you enter.
  • Anonymous device identifier — a random ID generated by Firebase Authentication that identifies your device session. It contains no personal information.

2. How We Use Your Information

All collected data is used solely to operate the App's core features: creating and joining draft rooms, running tournaments, and displaying standings. We do not sell, rent, or share your data with third parties for marketing purposes.

3. Data Storage

Tournament room data is stored in Google Firebase Firestore, a cloud database operated by Google LLC. By using the App you agree to Google's Firebase privacy terms. Data is stored on servers in the United States.

Your display name, settings, and tournament history are also stored locally on your device using AsyncStorage and are not transmitted unless you are actively in a tournament room.

4. Data Retention

Tournament rooms are automatically deleted from our servers after 30 days of inactivity. Local data on your device persists until you uninstall the App.

5. Children's Privacy

The App is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

6. Your Rights

You may request deletion of any data associated with your anonymous device ID by contacting us at the email below. Because we use anonymous identifiers rather than accounts, deleting and reinstalling the App also effectively removes your identity from our servers.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Continued use of the App after changes constitutes acceptance of the new policy.
