## Full Description (4000 chars max)
MTG Draft Forge is the all-in-one tournament companion for Magic: The Gathering players. Whether you're running a Friday Night Draft for four friends or a sixteen-player competitive event, Draft Forge handles everything from room creation to the final bracket match — so you can focus on playing.

**DRAFT ROOMS**
Create a room and share a six-character invite code with your players. Everyone joins on their own device — iPhone or Android — and sees the same room in real time. No accounts, no sign-up, no friction.

**TOURNAMENT FORMATS**
Choose the format that fits your night:
• Single Elimination — classic knockout brackets, auto-generated from seedings
• Round Robin — every player faces every other player; standings calculated automatically
• Two-Phase — a round-robin or fixed-games seeding phase feeds into a single-elimination bracket
• Swiss / Suggested — the app recommends the best format for your player count

**REAL-TIME SYNC**
Powered by Firebase, every change the host makes — starting the tournament, recording match results, advancing brackets — appears instantly on every player's device. No refreshing, no waiting.

**LIFE COUNTER**
Track life totals for up to six players simultaneously. Each player gets a colour-coded card with large +/- buttons, a compact multi-player view for small screens, and visual danger alerts when life drops to five or below. Supports any starting life total.

**DICE ROLLER**
Roll any die from d4 to d100. Roll multiple dice at once, see individual results and the total, and review your full roll history for the session. Haptic feedback on every roll.

**SEATING CHART**
Randomise draft pod seating with one tap. Seats are shuffled and displayed clearly so every player knows where to sit before the packs are opened.

**SCHEDULE & STANDINGS**
Round-robin schedules are generated automatically. Track wins, losses, points and tiebreakers in the live standings table throughout the event. When it's time for the bracket, seedings are set from standings — no manual entry.

**TOURNAMENT HISTORY**
Every completed tournament is saved to your profile with final standings, your finish position, the champion, and all match results. Build your record over time.

**NO ACCOUNT REQUIRED**
Draft Forge uses anonymous authentication. You never create an account or enter an email address. Your player name is the only thing you need to get started.
