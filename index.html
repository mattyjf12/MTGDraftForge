📜 Frequently Asked Questions
How do I invite friends to my draft room?

When you create a room, a 6-character invite code is generated automatically — for example, DRAFT7. Share that code with your playgroup verbally, via text, or take a screenshot.

Your friends open the app, tap Join Room, enter the code, and they'll appear in your player list instantly.

Can players on different phones join the same room?

Yes — that's the primary use case. Everyone at your table runs the app on their own device and joins via the invite code. The room creator manages starting the tournament and logging results.

What's the difference between the tournament formats?

See the Formats section below for a full breakdown. The quick version: Single Elimination is fastest, Round Robin is fairest for small groups, and MTGA Style mirrors the Arena draft experience (first to 7 wins).

If you're unsure, pick Suggested — the app automatically chooses the best format for your player count.

How does the MTGA Style format work?

Each player tracks their own match record independently. The first player to reach 7 wins is the tournament champion. Reaching 3 losses eliminates a player.

Players don't necessarily play each other directly — this format tracks individual performance across the event, matching the Arena Limited experience.

How are tiebreakers handled in standings?

Standings are sorted by Match Points (3 per win) first. If players are tied on points, the tiebreaker is their accumulated final life total from all matches — so winning with more life remaining gives a standings advantage.

This encourages playing aggressively even in lopsided games.

Do I need an account or internet connection?

No account is ever required. The app works fully offline — all room data is stored on your device.

Multi-device room syncing (players on different phones seeing the same live bracket) requires all players to be on the same local network or have internet access.

How do I randomize the draft seating?

Inside your active room, tap Seating from the tournament hub, then tap Randomize Seats. The app generates a circular seating diagram showing numbered positions around the table, with pack-passing direction indicated.

Tap the button again to re-randomize at any time before the draft starts.

Can I use the life counter without creating a tournament?

Yes — the Life Counter tab is fully standalone. Open it directly from the bottom navigation. You can track life for 1–6 players, choose your starting life total (20, 30, 40, or 50), and add or remove players at any time.

What dice are available in the dice roller?

The roller includes: d4, d6, d8, d10, d12, d20, and d100. You can roll up to 10 dice at once and see individual results alongside the total.

Rolling a Natural 20 or a Critical 1 on a d20 triggers a special alert — feel the moment.

I found a bug or have a feature request — where do I report it?

You can report bugs and suggest features on our GitHub Issues page. Please include your iOS version, a description of what happened, and steps to reproduce if possible.

Alternatively, email us at mtgdraftforge@gmail.com and we'll follow up directly.

🗂️ Tournament Formats
Format Best For How It Works
⚔️ Single Elim Any size, fast events One loss and you're out. Bracket advances winners each round.
🛡️ Double Elim 4–16 players Winners and losers brackets. Two losses required for elimination.
🔄 Round Robin 3–12 players Everyone plays everyone. Most match points wins overall.
🌱 Seeded Elim Ranked groups Single elimination with seeded bracket draw — top seeds are protected.
🃏 MTGA Style 2–8 players First to 7 wins is the champion. 3 losses = eliminated.
✨ Suggested Any size The app automatically picks the best format for your player count.

🔒 Privacy Policy

Overview

MTG Draft Forge ("the app") is designed with privacy as a default. We do not collect, sell, or share your personal data with third parties.

Data We Collect

The app does not require an account and does not collect any personally identifiable information. All data you enter — room names, player names, match results, life totals — is stored locally on your device only.

Data Storage

All tournament and room data is stored on-device using iOS local storage (AsyncStorage). This data never leaves your device unless you explicitly share it. Uninstalling the app permanently removes all stored data.

Analytics & Tracking

MTG Draft Forge does not use any analytics services, advertising SDKs, or tracking technologies. We do not track how you use the app.

Third-Party Services

The app does not integrate with any third-party advertising or analytics platforms. If you contact us via email, your email address is used solely to respond to your inquiry.

Children's Privacy

MTG Draft Forge does not knowingly collect information from children under 13. The app contains no account creation, social features, or data collection of any kind.

Changes to This Policy

Any material changes to this privacy policy will be reflected on this page with an updated date. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.

Contact

Questions about this privacy policy? Email us at mtgdraftforge@gmail.com.
